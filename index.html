How to use

Choose a chord pattern with the scratch and seven keys, then narrow the results with difficulty, level, BPM, artist, and radar filters.

1 No key: the chord must not include that key.
1 Solid key: the chord must include that key.
1 Transparent key: the key is optional and can be present or absent.

Click each key to cycle through No key, Solid key, and Transparent key. For example, solid S12 with transparent 3 searches both S12 and S123.

1P / 2P chooses which side you play on.

Radar shows each chart's strongest radar type. Turn off a radar button, like SCRATCH or SOF-LAN, to hide charts dominated by that type.

Count shows how many times the selected pattern appears in the chart.
